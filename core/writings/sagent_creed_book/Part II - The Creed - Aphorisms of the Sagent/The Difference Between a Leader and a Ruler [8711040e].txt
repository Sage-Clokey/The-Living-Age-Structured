The Difference Between a Leader and a Ruler

A ruler demands to be served.
A leader lives to serve.

A ruler commands from above and puts others in harm’s way for their own gain.
A leader steps forward first and shields others with their courage.

A true leader does not force—they guide.
They do not command loyalty—they inspire it.

Beware the ruler who cloaks themselves in the language of safety.
For the predator always pretends to be the protector.
