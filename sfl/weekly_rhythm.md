# Weekly Rhythm — State Director Cadence

> A forest doesn't grow by crisis management. It grows by rhythm — rain, sun, rest, repeat.
> This is your rain cycle. Same day, same time, same actions. 90 minutes per week maximum.

---

## The Rule: 90 Minutes, 3 Blocks

You're in your final quarter at UCSC and running the Living Age. You cannot give SFL unlimited time. So you give it **structured** time — and that structure makes it more effective, not less.

| Block | When | Duration | What |
|-------|------|----------|------|
| **Pulse Check** | Monday morning | 30 min | Review pipeline, plan the week |
| **Outreach Window** | Wednesday afternoon | 40 min | Make calls, send messages |
| **Log & Reflect** | Friday evening | 20 min | Update contact log, move pipeline stages |

---

## Monday: Pulse Check (30 min)

Open `outreach_pipeline.md`. Ask yourself:

1. **Who's in "Reaching Out"?** Anyone overdue for Attempt 2? Anyone I should move to Dormant?
2. **Who's in "In Conversation"?** Anyone I haven't followed up with in 7+ days?
3. **Who's ready to move up?** Anyone in Conversation who's done something → move to Active
4. **Is my Reaching Out list under 10?** If under 10, pull the next highest-priority person from the Queue
5. **Anything from the LC group chat I should respond to?**

Write down 3–5 specific actions for Wednesday. No more. Example:
- Call Sarah M. (Attempt 1)
- DM Jordan K. follow-up about tabling date
- Reply to Luis in group chat about flyer design
- Text Alex to check in (monthly Active check-in)

---

## Wednesday: Outreach Window (40 min)

Do the 3–5 actions from Monday. That's it.

**Order of operations:**
1. Phone calls first (hardest, most valuable — get them done while energy is high)
2. Personal DMs second
3. Group chat / email last

**After each contact:** Open `contact_log.md`, add one line. Don't batch this — log immediately while it's fresh.

**If you finish early:** Don't add more outreach. Use the remaining time to think about which campuses are emerging as anchors, or draft a message for the monthly LC call.

**If you don't finish:** That's fine. Move the remaining items to next Wednesday. Don't create a backlog spiral.

---

## Friday: Log & Reflect (20 min)

1. **Update `outreach_pipeline.md`:**
   - Move anyone who responded from Reaching Out → In Conversation
   - Move anyone who did something from In Conversation → Active
   - Move anyone with 2 failed attempts to Dormant
   - Update the Pipeline Summary counts at the bottom

2. **Quick reflect (in your head, not a document):**
   - What conversation went well this week? Why?
   - What felt forced? Why?
   - Am I overextended? (If Stages 2+3 > 18, stop adding to Queue pulls)

3. **If relevant:** Note anything worth sharing at the monthly LC call or in the group chat

---

## Monthly: LC Network Call (30 min)

- Scheduled same day/time each month
- Agenda: each LC shares one win and one challenge (2 min each). You share resources and upcoming opportunities. Open floor for 10 min
- Keep it to 30 min. Respect everyone's time
- Follow up individually with anyone who raised something that needs attention

---

## Monthly: Lautaro/Garreth Check-In (20 min)

- Update on pipeline numbers (they want metrics — give them metrics)
- Share one story that captures what's working (they want narrative too)
- Ask for anything you need: contacts, resources, support
- Raise any concerns early — don't let problems compound

---

## Time Budget

| Activity | Frequency | Time | Monthly Total |
|----------|-----------|------|---------------|
| Pulse Check | Weekly | 30 min | 2 hrs |
| Outreach Window | Weekly | 40 min | 2 hrs 40 min |
| Log & Reflect | Weekly | 20 min | 1 hr 20 min |
| LC Network Call | Monthly | 30 min | 30 min |
| Lautaro Check-In | Monthly | 20 min | 20 min |
| **Total** | | | **~7 hrs/month** |

Seven hours a month. That's it. Everything else — events, tabling, content creation — is Living Age work that *also* serves SFL, not SFL work that steals from the Living Age.

---

## Emergency Override

If something urgent comes up (SFL deadline, LC crisis, opportunity that expires):
- Handle it in the next available Outreach Window, not immediately
- Unless it truly can't wait 48 hours, in which case: handle it, then return to rhythm
- Never let one urgent thing destroy the weekly structure. The rhythm is the system. Protect it.
