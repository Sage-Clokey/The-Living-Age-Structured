I remember on January 21st of 2024 we were walking the dunes in Los Osos and you told me to feel some rocks you had found and you told me they were healing. I responded I think that's just you. I think you may have misunderstood what I meant. I didn't mean the rocks were just healing for you. I meant you were healing. Holding your hand is healing.
