"Nobody’s Right If Everybody’s Wrong" – The Illusion of Sides

"Nobody’s right if everybody’s wrong."
— Buffalo Springfield, "For What It’s Worth"

This line is a paradox, a challenge to the way people think about conflict. It reveals a deeper truth: when both sides believe they are absolutely right, the real truth often gets lost.


---

1. The Illusion of Sides

Society thrives on division—us vs. them, left vs. right, rebel vs. authority.

People shout over each other instead of listening.

But if everyone is convinced they have the absolute truth, who is actually seeking it?


The system doesn’t need to silence people if it can keep them fighting each other.


---

2. The Cycle of Chaos

When both sides demonize each other, neither is open to understanding.

Protest turns into anger, anger turns into chaos, chaos turns into control.

The system knows that keeping people divided keeps them distracted.


If everyone is too busy proving they’re right,
No one stops to ask who is controlling the game.


---

3. The True Enemy is the Illusion

The moment you believe your side is infallible, you stop questioning.

The real battle is not side vs. side—it’s control vs. awareness.

When both sides are convinced they are right, truth becomes irrelevant.


True wisdom is not about winning an argument—it’s about seeing beyond it.


---

Final Thought: Wake Up Before You’re Played

If nobody’s right when everybody’s wrong,
Then maybe the real issue isn’t who wins—
It’s who benefits from keeping the fight going.
