Vecna saw this world and was shown the truth by the mindflayer. That this world was just a conformist performance and a set based on made up rules. But he didn't see the full picture. He believed that if he was the one to create the illusion then he could operate in this world. But that is not the solution to a world based on lies. The solution to lie is the truth not another lie. When you find that the world is made up of made up rules don't become the new ruler of those made up rules discover the true rules. This is how you break the cycle of the rat race. Vecna saw the truth and choose the lie from the corrupted truth that the mindflayer showed him to emprison him into a lie he could bear unlike all the others. Sometimes certain people can't take the made up rulers so the lie they go for is believing they can make up the rules themselves but they can't that is just another lie. This is just a new set piece that is put up right after you destroyed the one you were able to see. So now the set piece seems real to you. You are more trapped than you were before.
