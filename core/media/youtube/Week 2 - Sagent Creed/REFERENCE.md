## What Is a Sagent?

> "Wisdom is the knowledge from God. I am an agent of that knowledge. I serve through those orders not any worldly commands. In order to be a Sagent you must first reject being an agent."

> "All agents have an enemy but a Sagent's enemy gains power through conflict. If you try to fight them of the world you will lose — you must fight them of the spirit."

> "I am a sage agent that will serve to bring this insight to the world. Sometimes the best way to help someone see what is in front of their eyes that they cannot see is to tell them a story about it."

> "A world ruled by fear and submission is a world unworthy of the people who inhabit it. A world ruled by wisdom and free will is the only path toward something greater. The Sagent does not seek power over others, nor submit to the false power of men, but instead follows the eternal path of truth."

> "Stoic Christian anarchist Sagent. Each of these all mean the same things. Leave what you can't control up to God and stay calm — for everything that happens is part of his plan."

## The Shaken Jar Parable

> "There are 100 red ants and 100 black ants in a jar. The jar is shook and the red ants think black is the enemy and the black ants think red is the enemy. But the real enemy is the one who shook the jar. The only question is: how do you unshake the jar?"

> "This story of the ants can be seen as a metaphor for conflict, where the red ants and black ants are caught up in fighting each other, but the true cause of the chaos is external, represented by the jar shaker."

> "Provide clarity and understanding: After the shaking stops, it's important to help the ants — metaphorically, people — realize that their enemy isn't each other, but the external force that created the confusion."

> "Ultimately, to 'unshake the jar' is to restore peace by eliminating the external cause of confusion, allowing the red and black ants to realize they were never enemies in the first place."

Because if you start with the pink cow the first one in the lead will gain the pink cow when the players stop playing for the false crowd. Infinite games favor principles and finite games favor sell outs. Play the infinite game even if it seems like a finite game."

> "For nine years, I have grown my hair, and when it reached the length where it began to curl, something changed. I gained insight. I saw through the illusions woven over people's eyes, the programming that controls the world."

## Long Hair and Inner Power

> "Long hair itself isn't the super power — it is the mentality needed to maintain it. You must just let it grow for years no matter the little thoughts of maybe I should cut — but you keep persistence and faith that letting it grow will be worth it. It is like Thor's hammer."

> "The Long Hair Principle: Long hair is not the source of power — it is the covenant of patience. It grows not in a day, but in the slow passing of years, through seasons of doubt and moments of temptation to cut it away."

> "The true strength is not in the strands, but in the mind that protects them. For the symbol can be broken, shorn, or stolen — but the mind that forged it cannot."

> "Not from hammers, or hair, or holy places. These are only training wheels — tools to direct a power that was already inside you. The symbol is not the source."

> "Thor's hammer was never his power, and when the winds of Ragnarok took both his hair and his weapon, he found the truth: the source was within. You may lose what the world sees, but if the discipline remains, the power remains."

> "'That hammer was to control your power — it was never your source of strength.' — Odin of Asgard"

> "Gumby is the embodiment of freedom and the ability to do what you choose. Clay has infinite possibilities and is moldable to be anything and do anything. It was a cartoon to show a structured world what freedom was."

> "Joe Clokey picked up his father's clay and tried to breathe the old magic back into it, to remind the world that imagination is sacred. But the machine that sells stories has no patience for souls — it only devours them."

> "Clokey the god of stories. Stories are a great way to change the world. Make stories for the story's sake not for financial gain and you will get the financial gain."

> "King Arthur, my grandfather was Arthur Clokey, my father was Joseph Arthur Clokey, and I am Sage Arthur Jordan Clokey."

> "I'm must finish what my grandfather started — but what really did he start? The cloak is not to hide but to show we are set apart from the world. I am the third Clokey, the heir of Arthur."

> "Clokey and the power of Clokey is to enter the beast without being corrupted by it but purify it to do its true purpose."

> "My grandfather, two great grandfathers named Joseph — one a professor and music composer and the other a Lutheran priest. Arthurs create after the Ragnarok and the Josephs continue until they are taken away in the Ragnarok."

## The Three-Act Narrative (Pledge, Turn, Prestige)

> "Every great story comes in three parts — the pledge, the turn and the prestige. The pledge and the prestige are almost the same but the prestige is done better because of what was done in the turn that allows the protagonist to actually solve the problem instead of fight it."

> "In the pledge you place all the dominoes. In the turn you flick one domino. And in the prestige you watch the show and see all your hard work come together to create something."

> "Every domino is important for the prestige to work — if one is missing it will stop the chain and it won't all be revealed. You must wait for the proper moment to turn. Make sure you set every Chekhov's gun so the 3rd act will work."

> "My 3 favorite things to talk about are money, politics and religion. Why are these things so taboo? Maybe because each of these things have been corrupted to control people... Money has become debt. Protection has become subjugation. And religion has become damnation. Each of these things need to be reborn in the prestige."

> "I am the prestige of Camelot — Sage Arthur."

> "The Saga Within: All great stories rhyme. They do not move in a straight line, but in spirals — echoing, repeating, unfolding until what was hidden becomes revealed. The Sagent walks this path not once, but again and again, each time deeper, wiser, closer to the center."
