The Pink Cow and the Source Unseen
A Sagent’s Wisdom on True Power, False Thrones, and the Courage to Stand Alone

Put not your trust in princes,
nor in a son of man, in whom there is no salvation.
When his breath departs, he returns to the earth;
on that very day, his plans perish.

Do not trust the throne, the robe, the title.
Systems built by man will fall like towers of sand.
Their temples may glitter and their banners wave,
but the truly Divine endures—unseen, unfazed, unshaken.

Asgard is not a place.
It is a people.

True power does not come from the things of this world.
Not from hammers, or hair, or holy places.
These are only training wheels—
tools to direct a power that was already inside you.
The symbol is not the source.
And when we confuse them, we become blind to the truth.

Sometimes you must be blinded to see.
Sometimes you must be broken to become whole.
For only when the illusion falls
do you realize the throne was empty,
and the crown was paper.

Blessed is the one whose help is in the Lord,
who keeps faith forever—
who executes justice for the oppressed,
feeds the hungry, lifts the bowed down,
frees the prisoner, loves the righteous.

And that is the power of one.
The power of the Pink Cow.

It only takes one to change everything.
One who dares to choose what is right
instead of what is popular.
One who stands alone
while the crowd moves as one.

Because the crowd plays a game.
And the game tells you:
"Win by guessing what others think."
"Score points by moving with the herd."
"Say what sounds right to others—
even if it’s not what you believe."

But the Sagent asks:
Should you play the game to win—
or play the game to do what is right?

I failed once.
I tried to get the points.
I tried to win the game the way they told me to.
But I wasn’t the real winner.
I traded truth for approval.
I betrayed what I knew was right
just to match the crowd.

Never again.

The Pink Cow is not about strategy.
It is about honor.
It is the symbol of thinking for yourself,
even when no one agrees.
It is the courage to say,
“This is what I believe,”
even if it costs you the crown.

And here is the hidden truth:

Courage is contagious.

Like the Armor Bearer who says,
“Do all that is in your heart. I am with you, heart and soul.”
That one act of bold loyalty
unlocks the courage of a leader.
And one leader standing firm
unlocks the courage of the crowd.

The Pink Cow is not just a rebellion.
It is a permission slip for others to be brave.

Because if you stay true to truth,
if you follow the divine within you,
the herd will follow in time.
Not because you forced them—
but because they saw something real in you.

Let the truth win through you.
Let wisdom speak through your silence.
Be the vessel,
and the Source will flow.
