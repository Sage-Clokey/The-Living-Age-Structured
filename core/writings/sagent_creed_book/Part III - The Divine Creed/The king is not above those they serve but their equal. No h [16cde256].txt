The king is not above those they serve but their equal. No heirarchy among humans only different roles. You can have leaders without them being rulers. This is the difference between true Zion and worldly Zion. Worldly Zion believes being choosen means you are above those not chosen but it doesn't mean that at all. Being choosen means you have a specific role but you are equal to all others. Matthew 20 25
