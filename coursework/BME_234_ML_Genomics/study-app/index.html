Five Course Topics

1. HMMs

Genome annotation — finding genes, CpG islands, chromatin states using hidden Markov models

2. Regression & GWAS

Logistic regression, association studies, eQTLs, TWAS, polygenic risk scores

3. ML & Variants

Decision trees, SVMs, random forests for classifying pathogenic vs benign variants

4. CNNs

Convolutional neural networks for predicting molecular phenotypes from DNA sequence

5. Language Models

Transformers, BERT-style models for DNA and protein sequences (ESM, AlphaFold)

Paper Discussions

ChromHMM

Ernst & Kellis 2010 — HMM-based chromatin state discovery from histone marks

COVID-19 GWAS

Genome-wide association study for COVID-19 severity

CADD

Combined Annotation Dependent Depletion — ML-based variant effect scoring

DeepSEA

CNN predicting chromatin effects of noncoding variants from sequence

AlphaGenome

Sequence-to-function model for regulatory genomics

AlphaFold

Deep learning protein structure prediction from amino acid sequence
