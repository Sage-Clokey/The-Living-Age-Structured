# Contact Log — Chronological Outreach Record

> Append-only. Never edit old entries. Just add new ones at the top.
> This is your memory. When you forget where you left off with someone, check here.

---

## How to Use

After every outreach — call, DM, email, in-person — add one line at the top. Takes 30 seconds. Saves you from the "wait, did I already message them?" spiral.

**Format:**
```
| Date | Name | Channel | Direction | Summary | Next Step |
```

- **Direction:** `→` (you reached out) or `←` (they reached out to you)
- **Summary:** One sentence. What happened
- **Next Step:** What you'll do next and by when
