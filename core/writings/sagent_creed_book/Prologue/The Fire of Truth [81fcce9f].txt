The Fire of Truth

Lies only spread by silencing those who tell the truth.
The truth can weather any resistance that may come to it,
but a lie withers with just one who dares to speak it.

Free discourse is the enemy of lies and the ally of truth.
Do not cut off those who tell you things that may be true,
even when those truths are the ones you do not want to hear—
for the comfortable lie will consume you.

This is why I left an ideology that could not take dissenting views.
They cut off those who do not toe the line,
because their beliefs cannot survive free discourse—
they break down when questioned.

I am Sage, the fire of truth that burns away the demons who tell lies.
This begins with asking questions—
the kind that pierce the core of the mass psychosis.
This is the purpose of the True Republic:
to question, to understand,
to lead others away from illusion and toward truth.

Lies can be most damaging to those who silence
the ones who question the lies they wish to believe.

The truth tellers never wanted to be right—
they simply saw things for what they were,
in ways others could not.
Sometimes the unveiling of truth is ugly,
but the damage caused by lies
can only be healed through revelation.

Truth tellers are called crazy
by those whose lies they expose.
The very people they wish to free
often fight them for revealing
the truth that destroys their comfort.

That is why truth is a flame—
not a consuming fire like lies,
but a purifying one.
It burns, it hurts,
but pain is the path to purification.
No pain, no gain.

Those who see reality for what it is—
who see the schemes of people ruled by darker forces—
have an obligation to speak.
Not everyone can see,
and many are vulnerable to manipulation
if the truth tellers stay silent out of fear.

Those who see true reality must reveal it,
so the world can protect itself
from the lies that control and enslave humanity.

But I cannot claim to be a truth teller
until I speak the most important truth of all—
the one I was too afraid to tell you.

I love you. Olivia
And I always have.

I just never had the courage to say it.
You blocked me when I was finally ready to tell you,
and then you found someone else.
But I still felt a tension—
a strange, trance-like energy between you and him
that I can’t explain in words.
It isn’t reason; it’s feeling.

I want you to be at peace.
This fracture happened because I was afraid—
afraid to tell you how I really felt,
even though I knew you felt the same.
Now everything is more complicated.
You told me you liked me,
but when it mattered most, I froze.

Now I don’t know how to proceed.
I just know I needed to tell you the truth,
even if there’s no way to send it directly.

Because truth left unspoken becomes a ghost—
and this is mine.
