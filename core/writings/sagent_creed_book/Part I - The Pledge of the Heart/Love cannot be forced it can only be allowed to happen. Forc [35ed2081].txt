Love cannot be forced it can only be allowed to happen. Forcing what isnt meant to be will never work. Manifestation isn't about making your will happen it is Aligning your will with fate. Submission to fate instead of trying to recreate fate to something that should never be.

Sometimes what shouldn't be must be sabotaged by fate so that what must happen will. 


Destiny is the destination your choice is the path you take to get there   

The Creed of Unforced Love

Love cannot be taken.
It can only be welcomed.
What is meant will find its way without chains;
what is not meant will wither if bound.

To force the heart is to strangle it.
To allow the heart is to free it.

Manifestation is not the forging of your own kingdom—
it is the bending of the knee to the kingdom already written.
Align your will with the river of fate,
and it will carry you to the shores prepared for you.

Do not try to redraw the map of destiny;
you will only lose your way.
What is yours will come in its season.
What is not will pass like leaves on the wind.
