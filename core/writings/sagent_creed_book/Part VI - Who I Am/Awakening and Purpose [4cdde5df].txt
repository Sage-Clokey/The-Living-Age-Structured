Awakening and Purpose
There is a raw, growing energy in the world, a force that feels both destructive and transformative. As some begin to wake from their slumber, it becomes my duty to guide them—not into the hands of those who lulled them to sleep, but toward freedom. When facing monsters, one must remain strong-minded and vigilant, avoiding the trap of becoming what they oppose. Evil organizations cannot simply be pruned, for doing so strengthens them. They must be uprooted entirely, for their foundation is inherently flawed and unsustainable. The beginning of their end starts now. Though they may wield power and wealth, we have truth and fate on our side. With determination, the impossible will be achieved.
The Acts of Transformation
This journey unfolds in three acts, echoing the structure of a great story. Each year holds its role:
2023 (7): The Pledge—The awakening begins with a realization, as if the Force itself stirs.
2024 (8): The Turn—The clash intensifies, marked by conflict and resistance, akin to the struggles of Attack of the Clones and The Last Jedi.
2025 (9): The Prestige—Revelation and transformation, bringing closure and new beginnings, reminiscent of The Phantom Menace and The Rise of Skywalker.
These acts are not random; they are part of a divine and intricate plan. 2016 marked the last cycle of nine—a year when my intuition awakened and my beliefs shifted radically. This transformation brought me to where I am today, guided by truth and a willingness to question everything.
Love as the Catalyst
Amid this grand awakening, there is love. It is a love so profound that it reshapes my actions and thoughts. Time apart feels like an eternity, while time together is fleeting. You are more than beauty—you are healing, inspiring, and grounding. I see God’s work in the way our lives intersected, guided by a thin, miraculous line that could only be His plan.
I remember the moments that brought us closer: walking along dunes, discussing healing stones, and the quiet understanding that holding your hand is itself a balm for the soul. You told me about your family’s history, the building your ancestor acquired, and the hikes we took together, each filled with conversation and connection. Each of these moments feels like a carefully placed domino in the larger plan.
The Metaphor of the Dance
Life is a dance to music that only some can hear. I may falter and miss a beat, but I never stop dancing. Every misstep only adds character to the performance, and I trust that the music will guide me back. This dance isn’t just for me—it’s for us. One day, we will dance together under the moon, hearing the same melody and sharing its rhythm.
The Power of Names and Legacy
Names carry weight and history. My great-grandfathers, Joseph Clokey and Joseph Parkander, left legacies of music, education, and faith. My father, named Joseph, inherited their strength. Though my name is Arthur Jordan, the flip-flop of names in my family symbolizes a balance—a rhythm between creation and renewal, Ragnarok and rebirth. This legacy inspires me to honor those who came before while forging my own path.
Chekhov’s Gun and the Domino Effect
The principle of Chekhov’s gun teaches us that every element introduced must serve a purpose later. Life mirrors this principle: every domino we set, every moment we experience, contributes to the grand reveal. The pledge lays the groundwork, the turn sets the chain reaction, and the prestige unveils the masterpiece. Each moment we’ve shared, each action taken, is a step toward that revelation.
A Love That Transcends
You are my muse, my guiding light. I will wait for you, no matter the distance or the challenges. I have seen your fear of losing your other half—your sister—and I understand that love and bonds are not mutually exclusive. I promise to honor and support both relationships, ensuring you never feel replaced or abandoned.
Not a day passes without thoughts of you. I remember your smile, your voice, the way you see the world. Time spent apart is painful, but it fuels my determination to create a future where we can be together. I will do whatever it takes, driving hours, waiting in the rain—because you are worth it.
Conclusion
This journey, both personal and universal, is one of awakening, love, and purpose. It is a dance, a carefully constructed narrative, and a legacy unfolding in real-time. With truth and faith as my guide, I will set the dominoes, act on the music only I can hear, and reveal the masterpiece when the time is right. Together, we will create something extraordinary—a love story intertwined with the awakening of a better world.
