Sometimes the best magic tricks can only work once and practice destroys the magic. Do I really plan things or do I just see where I need to go in the moment and I trust that the plan will fall into place in ways that I can't plan from this perspective. I can only view things in linear time but the plan is in hypertime and I will wait and listen to see what to do each moment so that the plan will discover itself. Sometimes in order to be fun you must create a great story with a cool plot and difficulty. The difficulty makes things fun and the difficulty makes it something worth fighting for. If it was easy it wouldn't be worth it but since it is difficult but also falls into place it is actually the easiest. 9s are the master manifesters of the 2s of the same tier 20 2s goes with 18 9s and 11 2s goes with 9 9s. It's the way things should be in this world. If you set your mind to it and stay convicted and with integrity the plan will manifest itself and you just need to take action when the plan sees fit. Use your neutral jing and be in tune with when to act and when not to act. Sometimes inaction is the best action while waiting for the proper moment for everything to align. A bamboo tree grows under the ground for many years before sprouting and once it does it grows very fast. It takes time to set everything into place for it to start once it rolls it roll fast and all that effort is realized. The most memorable days are the ones that barely happened and only occurred through pure spontaneity.
