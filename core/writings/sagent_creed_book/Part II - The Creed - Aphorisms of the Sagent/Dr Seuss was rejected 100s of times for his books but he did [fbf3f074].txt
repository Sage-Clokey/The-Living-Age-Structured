Dr Seuss was rejected 100s of times for his books but he didn't change the book. Stan Lee created spider man and was rejected at first but became his most famous character. If you believe something is right you can make it happen even after it is rejected the first few times but when the time is right it will happen. Sometimes the greatest things are rejected at first and the worst things are accepted. A muscle must break in order for it to grow. A muscle that has never been broken will always be weak and will not function for it's true purpose.
