You only need one piece to win at chess. It all about placement. You may take more pieces but i am in a better position. Chess isn't about taking out more pieces. it is about taking out the king. When I play I play to win the war not every battle. Sometimes you can win every battle but loose the war. Focus on what wins the war not what seems to be the short term gains. Focus on what wins the war not what wins the battle. Every sacrifice moves closer to the end game where the Jedi knight checkmates the emperor.
