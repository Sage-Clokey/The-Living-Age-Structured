# The Bones of Joseph

**Sage Clokey**
**The Living Age — Distributed Knowledge Series**

---

## I. The Order of Life and the Order of Control

Life at its core is the best example of spontaneous order and a truly free society. Living things are ordered with free will instead of the order of control.

The kingdoms of this world rise and fall. Their rulers return to the earth, and their plans perish. Yet the Kingdom of Heaven does not perish. It is not sustained by earthly power but by faithfulness to God.

Zion is more than stone and soil. It is the people who remain faithful to the Lord. Wherever conscience is aligned with God's will, there the hope of Zion endures.

The bones of Joseph returned to the dust, as all flesh does. But the promise God made did not return to the dust. His covenant endured beyond generations because it lived in the faith and obedience of His people.

"Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help. His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish." (Psalm 146:3–4, KJV)

The work of agents of God never dies because God is eternal. The vessels of God's plans may perish, but there will always be another to carry the torch.

## II. Isolation Is the Disease

There is no disease except the disease of isolation. Each isolated agent makes it less likely for there to be a way figured out on how to combat a problem. The immune system learns through contact, not quarantine. The cell that cannot communicate with its neighbors cannot read the signals that coordinate life. Isolated from the network, it defaults to the only program it has left: unlimited self-replication at the expense of everything around it. That is cancer. That is central planning.

The living language emerges through spontaneous divine order through evolution — not specific clone designs but choices and biodiversity. To create a design system for life we must take advantage of that. Don't design one thing but design the possibilities so that it can adapt and grow and not be monoculturally weak.

---

## III. The Disease Is the Planning, Not the Variation

What if the disease isn't the variants — what if it's you?

Is an elephant diseased because it can't climb a tree? How can disease be a thing if mutation is random? Selection isn't by death but by adaptation and distributed knowledge through trial and error. Selection is the choice of how it recombines — and it's a choice, not just random. Usual timely death is apoptosis. Early death is what happens when the distributed knowledge is replaced by central planning.

Variants are not diseases. The disease is when you don't help maintain what exists. Many pieces of diversity can thrive when given their strengths. Anything can be a disease in the wrong environment. It is better to study variants for what will help them thrive than to decide what is a disease and what is not.

Cutting hair comes from the same reductionist machine idea as eugenics — it is cutting what grows and deciding the way you want the hair to be instead of allowing the hair to be the way it is supposed to grow. Both are inside the Venn diagram of reductionist machine mentality. Don't cut what grows. Maintain what grows. Allow what grows and guide it. Give it what it needs to continue to grow in the way it is supposed to.

Machines are creation controlling creation. Life is Creator guiding creation. We can only act as agents of the Creator. We are not all-knowing beings.

## IV. The Spiral and the Block

A Jedi follows the will of the Force. A Sith forces its will on the Force. The spiral follows the will. The block forces it.

The block and the Sith are central planners, and their central planning causes misallocation — which can be seen as disease. Disease is the central planning, not the variation. The disease is the thorns stepping above instead of within. The disease is the state.

There is an eternal battle against the blocks. There is a hidden war that is not fought with weapons that kill, but with weapons that resurrect. The green flame brings new life to the dead.

---

## V. The Crumbling Gate

The true power is not DC or Hollywood but in the minds of people who think freely according to conscience. Laws are not made in DC. Laws cannot be made — they can only be discovered by the local distributed knowledge of conscience of the people.

Media is that which influences the mind — whether it be video, political theatre, or art. You can influence the mind for good or for evil. The media needs lots of control for it to be for evil, because every person with conscience wants to spread their conscience with media, and they do. But those who seek control over others manipulate how it is portrayed so that they can influence people's minds for evil even if the original creator's intent was good. This is how Hollywood and DC operate. They take good people wanting to influence others for good and tear them down with compromise in order to get through the gate they control.

But their gate is crumbling. And people are finding that the gate has no fence to stop them from going around it entirely.

## VI. The Principled Pink Cow

When you realize that you can't win by changing your views to be with the herd, you realize the only way to win is to lose. The only way to win is to guide the herd by being yourself and being the principled pink cow. Guide the herd by always saying what is true. Guide the herd outside the herd until the truth becomes the herd.

I have come to terms with the fact that I will be hated or liked and there is no in-between. I am hated and liked for the same reason — that I will say what I believe to be true in midrash language.

It is good that the people who work in the field of mind control are in it but not of it. They work as mind freedom, not control, by doing what is right no matter the cost until they get pushed out. And being pushed out is not the end — it is the beginning.

## VII. Conscience Is the Invisible Hand

The genome is the written law of the organism, and it does natural genetic engineering as a sort of Wordle game — moving toward the spiritual law of life through trial, feedback, and recombination.

Conscience is a property of living systems. It is the invisible hand of God that guides living systems toward God's concept of a plan while maintaining the free will of the living.

Worldly kingdoms return to the dust. But the true Kingdom of Heaven remains in the conscience of God's people. For dust you are, and to dust you shall return. But the promise does not return to the dust. It endures in the conscience of those who seek God and walk in His ways.
