"compassion is a sign of weakness" this is wrong. Compassion is a sign of strength. The lack of compassion is a weakness based on fear. It is based on the fear that if you are compassionate you won't be able to take out your enemies before they take you. Compassion required mental strength and the realization that it is impossible to fight evil in the way that evil fights good.
