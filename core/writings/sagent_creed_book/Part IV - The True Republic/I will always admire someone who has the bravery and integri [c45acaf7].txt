I will always admire someone who has the bravery and integrity to say and do what they believe to be true and good up to their death. Charlie Kirk was one of these people. Charlie wasn't perfect but no one is. He was in favor of open discussion to find truth not just to win the argument because he knew this. I don't agree with everything he has said but I agree with a lot of it when it is in favor of liberty. He will be missed. Rest in peace Charlie Kirk.
