You can't protect someone or something if it is you that are a threat to them. The first thing needed to protect someone is to not harm or threaten them yourself. Then after that you can protect them from others



---

The Foundation of True Protection

You cannot protect someone or something if you, yourself, are a threat to them. The very first step in being a protector is to ensure that you are not the one causing harm. True protection starts with self-awareness and restraint, understanding that your actions must be aligned with peace and safety, not conflict. Only after you have mastered this can you truly begin to protect others—from the external dangers that may threaten them.

In this sense, the role of a protector is not just one of action, but one of responsibility and self-discipline. It is only when we cease to be a threat ourselves that we can stand as guardians, offering safety and security to those who need it most.


---

This emphasizes the importance of inner alignment and responsibility before being able to outwardly protect others.
