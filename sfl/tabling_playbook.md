# Tabling Playbook — The Spiral, Not the Pamphlet

> Don't ask "Are you a libertarian?" Ask questions that make people discover the answer themselves.

---

## The Approach

Traditional liberty tabling: banners, pamphlets, "big government bad." It filters for people who already agree. It converts no one.

Spiral tabling: ask questions rooted in nature, biology, and common experience. Let people walk into the insight. They remember what they discover far longer than what they're told.

---

## Opening Questions (Pick 1–2 per session)

### The Forest Question
**"How does a forest organize itself without a manager?"**
- No tree tells another where to grow
- Mycelium networks share resources underground without central allocation
- The forest is more resilient than any plantation
- Follow-up: "What if we organized communities the same way?"

### The Body Question
**"What if your body ran like a government?"**
- 37 trillion cells, no hierarchy, no central planning office
- Your immune system doesn't wait for approval to respond to a threat
- Cells that try to centralize control? That's cancer
- Follow-up: "So why do we think society needs what biology rejected?"

### The Shaken Jar
**"Have you heard about the red ants and the black ants?"**
- Put red ants and black ants in a jar. They coexist
- Shake the jar. They attack each other
- They think the enemy is the other color. The real enemy is whoever shook the jar
- Follow-up: "Who's shaking our jar right now?"

### The Spiral vs Block
**Draw it on a whiteboard or paper:**
- The block: grid, hierarchy, top-down, rigid. Every empire in history
- The spiral: Fibonacci, emergence, bottom-up, adaptive. Every living system in nature
- "Which one are you living in? Which one do you want?"

## One-Page Handout: "5 Things Your Body Knows About Freedom"

1. **No cell is in charge.** 37 trillion cells coordinate without a president, a congress, or a central plan. They respond to local signals, not top-down commands.

2. **Centralized control is literally cancer.** When one cell overrides the cooperative system and demands all resources flow to it, we call that a tumor.

3. **Your immune system doesn't ask permission.** It detects threats and responds immediately — no bureaucratic approval chain. Decentralized defense works.

4. **Diversity is resilience.** Monocultures collapse. Ecosystems with diverse, independent actors survive anything. The same is true for economies and communities.

5. **Life grows in spirals, not grids.** From galaxies to seashells to DNA — the pattern is the Fibonacci spiral. Emergence, not engineering. The question is: why do we keep trying to organize society like a grid when everything alive uses spirals?

## Conversation Flow

1. **Open with a question** (from above). Don't explain — ask
2. **Listen.** Let them think out loud. Most people have never been asked these questions
3. **Connect to their major/interests.** Bio student? Talk PPI networks. Business student? Talk markets as emergent order. Philosophy? Talk conscience vs authority
4. **Don't close with ideology.** Close with: "If this was interesting, here's where we go deeper" — hand them the QR code or sign-up sheet
5. **Note the ones who light up.** Those are your future LCs

---

## What NOT to Do at the Table

- Don't argue. If someone disagrees, ask another question. The enemy gains power through conflict
- Don't use jargon: "NAP," "praxeology," "Austrian economics" — these are insider language that excludes
- Don't trash-talk other political positions. The shaken jar parable applies here too
- Don't table alone if possible — two people creates natural conversation energy
- Don't measure success by sign-ups. Measure it by the quality of conversations and the people who come back
