# True Republic — Source Reference Notes
Direct quotes and ideas from Sage's writings to draw from when writing scripts.

## What a True Republic Actually Is

> "A true Republic is not a form of government or a state with the monopoly on legitimate use of force but a network of voluntary action where each individual follows their own conscience instead of arbitrary rules from human hierarchy."

> "The True Republic is born not from coercion, but from conversation. Not from authority, but from inquiry. Not from obedience, but from awareness."

> "This is the purpose of the true Republic — to question the beliefs of people and work through what people think and why and lead them towards the truth and away from lies."

> "Lies can be most damaging to those who cut off the ones who question the lies they wish to be true."

## Government, Force, and Authority

> "Persuasion is just, but the initiation of force is not. A just society cannot be built upon coercion, for the moment force replaces reason, truth is abandoned."

> "No one group should have the monopoly on the right to use violence. Each person has the right to use violence in very specific cases. This is the defense of others and themselves and their property."

> "Enforcement of an unjust law is worse than criminal because it uses the facade of fighting criminals to commit their crimes. A crime isn't breaking the worldly law scribbles — it is breaking natural law."

> "The legislation is with everyone. It is not what happens in some Roman-style building that created laws — God created law and wrote it on our hearts."

> "Better to be a criminal for doing what is right than follow the law by committing an atrocity."

> "It is every person's duty to uphold what is right in all they do. This must always be the highest priority — never to be sacrificed for power, wealth, or fame."

> "If the command of men contradicts the command of God, follow God. If the command of men aligns with the command of God, follow God still. Right action is not determined by human decree but by eternal truth."

## The Ruler King vs. The Leader King

> "The ruler king is an agent of satan and the leader king is an agent of god. The ruler is there to be served and the leader is there to serve and lead others to do the same."

## The Shaken Jar — Manufactured Division

> "There are 100 red ants and 100 black ants in a jar. The jar is shook and the red ants think black is the enemy and the black ants think red is the enemy. But the real enemy is the one who shook the jar. The only question is: how do you unshake the jar?"

> "This story of the ants can be seen as a metaphor for conflict, where the red ants and black ants are caught up in fighting each other, but the true cause of the chaos is external, represented by the jar shaker."

> "To 'unshake the jar' means to address the root cause of the chaos... the solution lies in calming or neutralizing this external disturbance."

> "Ultimately, to 'unshake the jar' is to restore peace by eliminating the external cause of confusion, allowing the red and black ants to realize they were never enemies in the first place."

## Lies and Truth in a Society of Mass Psychosis

> "When in a society of mass psychosis, seeing unapologetic reality can seem like psychosis."

> "The Fire of Truth: Lies only spread by silencing those who dare to speak the truth. A truthful idea can weather any storm — resistance only sharpens and strengthens it. A lie, however, survives only as long as no one speaks the single fact that exposes it."

> "Free discourse is the ally of truth and the mortal enemy of deception. Whenever voices are censored, silenced, shamed, or exiled for asking questions, we discover a truth far more revealing than any answer: the lie cannot survive scrutiny."

> "The world becomes a prison not because of the strength of the liars, but because of the silence of the honest."

> "Truth is the flame that frees the world."

## Conscience, Morality, and Moral Law

> "What is the difference between humans and animals? A conscience. The intuition to know what is right and wrong."

> "In order to sin you must have a conscience that gives you the knowledge of good and evil. In order to do good you must have a conscience."

> "The true obligation of each person is to do what is right not what is legal. What is right never changes but it is discovered over time. What is right must be discovered not created. Man cannot change right and wrong just as man cannot change gravity."

> "When good men are convinced that good soldiers follow orders no matter what, they are no longer good protectors but weapons of destruction."

> "You can only serve one master and that should be God. God is the only master you can serve and not lose your master. If you serve money you lose money. If you serve fame you lose fame. If you serve winning you lose."

> "The True Republic Campaign: Simple. Clear. Uncompromising. End all force except defensive force. Absolute right to self-ownership. Freedom of speech — no censorship. Freedom of religion and conscience. End government monopolies on law, force, and authority."
