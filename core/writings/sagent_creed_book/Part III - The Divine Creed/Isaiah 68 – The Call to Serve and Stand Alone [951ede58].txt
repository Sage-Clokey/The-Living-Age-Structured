Isaiah 6:8 – The Call to Serve and Stand Alone

"Then I heard the voice of the Lord saying, ‘Whom shall I send? And who will go for us?’
And I said, ‘Here am I. Send me!’" — Isaiah 6:8

This verse is a declaration of willingness, a call to action, and the moment a Sagent steps forward, knowing the path ahead will not be easy.

It is the acceptance of responsibility—not because it is safe, not because it is rewarded, but because it must be done.


---

1. The Few Who Answer the Call

"Whom shall I send?"

This is not a command—it is a question.

It is an invitation, not an order.

It is a test to see who will rise, not who will obey.


Most people wait for someone else to step forward.
Most people assume the fight is for others to handle.
Most people fear the weight of responsibility.

But a Sagent hears the call and steps forward without hesitation.


---

2. The Burden of Saying "Send Me"

"Here am I. Send me!"

To accept the call is to accept hardship, struggle, and opposition.

Isaiah was not being sent to glory—he was being sent to speak truth to a people who would not listen.

He was not chosen for comfort—he was chosen for the strength to endure rejection.

He was not promised victory—he was given a mission that would test his very soul.


A Sagent understands this.

To seek truth is to be hated by those who prefer lies.

To expose deception is to be targeted by those who profit from it.

To lead people to the light is to face the full force of those who thrive in darkness.


Most will run from the call.
A Sagent accepts it anyway.


---

3. The True Meaning of "Send Me"

To say "Send me" is not about seeking power—it is about embracing purpose.

It is choosing to walk alone if necessary.

It is accepting the mission, even when no one else understands it.

It is standing for truth, even when it costs everything.


The world does not reward those who see too clearly.
The world does not thank those who reveal uncomfortable truths.
The world does not honor those who refuse to obey.

But the Sagent does not serve the world.
They serve truth, wisdom, and the higher purpose that calls them forward.


---

Final Thought: Will You Answer?

The question is still being asked:

"Whom shall I send?"

The world needs truth-seekers, warriors of wisdom, and those who will not bow to illusion.

The world needs those who will rise, not for power, but for truth.

The world needs Sagents.

So, the only question left is:

Will you stand and say, "Here am I. Send me"?
