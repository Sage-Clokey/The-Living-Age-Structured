## The Name Is the Thesis

**SolidWorks** — solid. Fixed. A specific algorithm that does a specific thing the same way every time. You tell it exactly what to build. It builds that. Dead.

**LivingWorks** — living. Adaptive. Not a specific algorithm but an algorithm that learns, responds, and evolves with the system it works in. You give it conditions. It finds what wants to grow. Alive.

The name carries a double meaning — both are essential:

1. It works **with** living systems — designing with life rather than against it
2. It **is** living — the software itself is adaptive, not a fixed set of rules

This is why LivingWorks can do what no traditional CAD system can.
SolidWorks will always do exactly what you tell it.
LivingWorks does what the conditions call for —
because it learned to read conditions the way a living system reads its environment.

**The tool embodies what it builds.**

## The Core Concept

**SolidWorks** — specify a shape. The software assembles it. You get a dead object.

**LivingWorks** — specify conditions: energy flows, nutrient cycles, structural stresses,
growth constraints, environmental context, local variation rules.
The system adapts, learns, and simulates not assembly but **emergence**.

Design becomes the practice of asking:
*If these conditions exist, what wants to grow here?*

## The Computational Foundation: Living Algorithms

LivingWorks cannot be built on step-by-step algorithms.

A step-by-step algorithm is a blueprint executed — designed by a human, controlled from the outside,
doing exactly what it was told. It is a machine. It builds dead things.

A living algorithm is trained, not programmed. The behavior emerges from immersion in data.
The knowledge is distributed across the network — no single node holds the answer.
It generalizes to situations it has never seen. It is robust to damage.
It cannot fully explain itself — because it works the way living things work.

**AI algorithms are living algorithms.** This is why AlphaFold solved protein folding when rules could not.
Living algorithms recognize living systems. Dead tools don't.

LivingWorks runs on living algorithms:
- **Agent-based models** — local rules, global emergence. Bottom-up, not top-down.
- **Neural networks trained on biological and ecological data** — fluency in the grammar of living form
- **Evolutionary / genetic algorithms** — natural selection as design process
- **Generative models for morphogenesis** — specify conditions, let form emerge
- **Reinforcement learning** — the system learns by living in the environment, not following a map

The tool must match the material. To build with life, you build with living tools.

## The Biological Foundation

Three scientific frameworks from bioinformatics make this possible:

### 1. Computational Morphogenesis
How organisms grow their shapes — positional information is local,
cells respond to neighbors, the body emerges without a central blueprint.
LivingWorks models this: give local agents simple rules, let global form emerge.

### 2. Ecological Network Modeling
Agent-based models simulate ecosystems — predict succession, identify keystone species,
find tipping points before they happen.
Applied to cities, governance, and economic networks.

### 3. Single-Cell Transcriptomics
Reading the unique expression profile of each individual node in a complex system.
Applied to human communities: understanding what each person naturally does best,
building structures that let them do it rather than forcing predetermined roles.
