Cells act by choice and they signal other cells but they only react to those signals by choice. There are cases where some coerce and that is called pathogens, viruses, and cancer. These cells have the idea that it is better to die than be coerced. The immune system is the defense of the voluntary order. It is the model of how defense works in a voluntaryist world from the molecular all the way up to the individual and the society.

The genome is the written law of the organism and it does natural genetic engineering as a sort of Wordle game to move toward the spiritual law of life.

The living language emerges through spontaneous divine order through evolution — not specific clone designs but choices and biodiversity. To create a design system for life we must take advantage of that. Don't design one thing but design the possibilities so that it can adapt and grow and not be monoculturally weak.
