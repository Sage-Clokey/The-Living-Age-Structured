"I'm the Rick, and So Were the Rest of You" – The Sagent's Warning

Power corrupts, but the desire for control corrupts even faster.

Once, we all saw through the illusion.
We rejected authority, the system, the chains of control.
We understood that no one has the right to rule over others.

But then—
Fear crept in.
Comfort became more appealing than chaos.
And those who once fought the system began to recreate it.

Just like Rick warns his fellow Ricks—
“You hated the government, so you became the government.”

This is the cycle of every revolution that loses sight of its purpose.


---

The Paradox of Rebellion – When Freedom Becomes Authority

The rebels become the rulers.

The revolutionaries become the bureaucrats.

The anti-system thinkers create a new system that looks just like the old one.


History repeats not because people forget the past,
But because they are too afraid to live without control.

They tell themselves,
"We just need structure. We just need to keep order."
And before they know it, they have built exactly what they swore to destroy.


---

The Sagent’s Warning – Stay Free or Become the Enemy

If you fight for freedom, but fear uncertainty, you will create a new prison.

If you destroy the system only to replace it with another, you have done nothing.

If you reject authority only to seize it for yourself, you were never truly free.


True rebellion is not about taking power.
It is about refusing to recreate the chains.

Because the moment you start saying,
"We just need to organize, we just need to govern, we just need to maintain control…"

You are no longer the rebel.
You are no longer the free thinker.
You are the new system.

And someone else, somewhere, is looking at you and saying—
"I'm the Rick, and so were the rest of you."
