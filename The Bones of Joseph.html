Life and economics are the study of the same living spontaneous order.

The Living Age
☙ ☙ ☙

Life is voluntary order guided by conscience, and everything that replaces conscience with control — whether at the cellular level or the political level — is the disease.

The Living Age rests upon three complementary understandings. Black — economics: no worldly rulers, spontaneous order, freedom creates coordination. Purple — theology: the true divine King, conscience guides moral responsibility, freedom is preserved while goodness is invited. Green — biology: living order, life continually adapts through distributed communication, living systems organize themselves. These are not separate truths. Economics explains how free societies organize. Theology explains why conscience matters. Biology explains how living systems grow. Together they suggest one living principle: order emerges through distributed agents acting according to conscience rather than through centralized control.

Adam Smith described the invisible hand of the market — a spontaneous order emerging from countless individuals making voluntary decisions with only local knowledge. There is another invisible hand. Conscience is the invisible hand of God. Just as decentralized markets coordinate millions of independent decisions into an economic order, conscience coordinates the free actions of living beings into a living order.

There are two orders in this world, and there have always been two. One is the order of life — spontaneous, voluntary, free. The other is the order of control — imposed, mechanical, dead.

The spiral is the shape of a system with God as its king. Nature does not grow in cubes. Trees do not branch in right angles. Shells do not expand in grids. Galaxies do not rotate in boxes. The Fibonacci spiral appears again and again because it is the geometry of efficient, decentralized growth. No central planner commands the spiral. Each part grows according to local information, and the whole emerges naturally.

This is what Hayek called spontaneous order — the pattern that arises when distributed agents coordinate through local signals that no planner designed and no authority commands. He described it in markets. But the cell discovered it four billion years before anyone wrote it down. The E. coli gene regulatory network — 282 nodes, 308 edges — tolerates removal of 36.8% of its nodes before fragmenting. A centralized star graph collapses at 1.9%. That is a 19:1 robustness ratio in favor of the distributed architecture. The spiral is not just beautiful. It is measurably superior by an order of magnitude.

The machine is the opposite philosophy. It is creation forcing its will on creation. It is reductionism to the point that the parts are brittle — what was once strong is now weak. Don't cut what grows. Maintain what grows. Allow what grows and guide it. Give it what it needs to continue in the way it is supposed to. Machines are creation controlling creation. Life is Creator guiding creation. We can only act as agents of the Creator. We are not all-knowing beings.

Voluntaryism is written into how biology functions. Cells act by choice. They signal other cells, but they only react to those signals by choice. Every gene transfer, every vesicle received, every nanotube built requires both sides to participate. The membrane is the property line. The receptor is the customs agency. Nothing enters without consent.

There are two ways things are made: top-down control — machines, factories, monoculture — and bottom-up emergence — biology, ecosystems, markets. Free markets mirror living systems. Central planning mirrors mechanical systems. Mises argued that without prices, a central planner cannot rationally allocate resources. The cell proves him right. Each enzyme prices its substrate through binding affinity. Each receptor prices its ligand through expression level. ATP emerged as the universal biological currency the same way money emerges in Menger's theory — not by design, but because adenosine triphosphate happened to be the most exchangeable energy carrier, the most "saleable" metabolite, and every pathway that could use it gained a selective advantage. The cell runs a price system older than civilization. In simulation, distributed metabolic allocation retains 71.1% of output under perturbation. Centralized allocation retains 53.0%. Even an omniscient linear program — flux balance analysis with a complete genome-scale model of 2,712 reactions and 1,516 genes — achieves only 70% accuracy on gene knockout predictions, because 30% of the knowledge exists only in the local state of each molecular agent and cannot be encoded in any central model. This is Mises' calculation problem confirmed in metabolites. And when that price system breaks — when a cell stops reading growth factor signals, ignores contact inhibition, overrides the feedback that says stop — you get cancer. Cancer is not a disease of bad parts. It is the destruction of the price system that coordinated voluntary exchange.

The genome is the written law of the organism, and it does natural genetic engineering as a sort of Wordle game — moving toward the spiritual law of life through trial, feedback, and recombination. Selection isn't by death but by adaptation and distributed knowledge, by trial and error. Selection is the choice of how it recombines — and it's a choice, not just random. The living language of genetics emerges through spontaneous divine order — not specific clone designs but choices and biodiversity. Don't design one thing. Design the possibilities so that it can adapt and grow and not be monoculturally weak.

Empire cannot tolerate living systems. To control a system, you must first kill it. Living ecosystems are too complex, too adaptive, too uncontrollable. So empire clears them. Monoculture replaces biodiversity. Concrete replaces soil. Bureaucracy replaces wisdom.

There is no disease except the disease of isolation. Each isolated agent makes it less likely for there to be a way figured out on how to combat a problem. This is Hayek's knowledge problem made flesh. The immune system's cell-to-cell communication network has a betweenness Gini coefficient of 0.000 — perfectly distributed, zero gatekeeping. Remove any single cell type and 75% of communication survives. No master cell. No central authority deciding which signals are permitted. Every agent you isolate is a node removed from that network. Each one carried local knowledge — immune experience, behavioral adaptation, signal processing that no other node possessed. The network doesn't just lose a participant. It loses what that participant would have discovered, shared, and contributed to the collective solution.

What if the disease isn't the variants — what if it's you? Is an elephant diseased because it can't climb a tree? Variants are not diseases. The disease is when you don't help maintain what exists. Many pieces of diversity can thrive when given their strengths. Anything can be a disease in the wrong environment. It is better to study variants for what will help them thrive than to decide what is a disease and what is not. Disease is the central planning, not the variation. The disease is the thorns stepping above instead of within.

I knew this before I had the data.

My first year at UCSC I was suspended for a year because I wouldn't wear a mask during the covid rules. I would go to the dining halls not wearing a mask because it made no sense to me why I would wear a mask when I would eat food in the same place. On the last day of my first quarter the dining hall called the campus police on me for just not wearing a mask — in a room where once people sat down they took it off and ate food.

I broke those rules instinctually, and I have come to realize that my instinct was aligned with the way that immunity to disease actually works. The immune system does not learn in quarantine. It learns through contact. Regulatory T cells develop tolerance by encountering antigens. Mucosal immunity builds its library by sampling what comes through, not by sealing the border. Steve Cole at UCLA has shown that social isolation literally reprograms gene expression — upregulating inflammation, downregulating antiviral defense. The genome reads isolation as pathology. The lockdowns did at the population level exactly what Cole measured at the genomic level: isolation in, antiviral defense out.

Beware the ruler who cloaks themselves in the language of safety. For the predator always pretends to be the protector. Tyranny never arrives as a threat — it arrives as a friend. As a savior. As the one who just wants to keep you safe.

The Kingdom of God is not of this world. It is written on the soul. It flows through the conscience. It is the Law that predates all kings, all flags, all papers, and all crowns. It is accessed through surrender — to the Source, to the Light, to the Force that moves without coercion. In this kingdom, no man is above another. Every soul is accountable directly to God. There is no chain of command — only a divine current through the conscience. Surrender to God, so you never kneel to man.

America is not a specifically Christian nation. It is a nation of conscience no matter the religion. Faith isn't in the specific story or the specific text or scripture — it is in man's relationship to its Creator, choosing to do what is right. Our soul is in our keeping alone. Not in kings, not priests, not popes or rabbis or gurus or sages — but each man who acts. You cannot say "but I was told by others to do otherwise." That will not suffice.

All written law is a guess at the law written on our heart. Do not follow the written law blindly — follow your conscience. It is every man's obligation to choose to do what is right, not to follow their leaders, because their leader could be wrong and it is your job to help them keep the right path by always choosing to do what is right. There is a higher order than the written order — the unwritten spiritual order that we must all move towards the best we can, and the only way to achieve it is to all act as distributed agents, not a hierarchy of chain of command.

The kingdom of conscience is one big distributed decentralized game of Wordle. This is the living Republic — the living kingdom of conscience, where laws follow the consciences of man, not the dictates of posers. America was started by people who believed that faith was more important than the specific story or sect. The scriptures and stories matter in so far as they tell how to have faith — not in believing the story itself, but in acting out the moral of the story.

When you realize that you can't win by changing your views to be with the herd, you realize the only way to win is to lose. The only way to win is to guide the herd by being yourself — the principled pink cow. Guide the herd by always saying what is true. Guide the herd outside the herd until the truth becomes the herd.

The true power is not in the capitals but in the minds of people who think freely according to conscience. Laws cannot be made — they can only be discovered by the local distributed knowledge of conscience of the people. Media is that which influences the mind, and it needs immense control for it to serve evil, because every person with conscience wants to spread truth and they do. But those who seek power over others control how it is portrayed, tearing good people down with compromise in order to get through the gate they control. Their gate is crumbling. People are finding that the gate has no fence to stop them from going around it entirely.

Being pushed out is not the end — it is the beginning. Where God is King, order grows. Where men claim the throne, order must be enforced.

There is an eternal battle against the blocks. There is a hidden war that is not fought with weapons that kill, but with weapons that resurrect.

The blue flame is the inheritance — the call to action, the fight against darkness, the warrior's path, the duty received. The red flame is the fall — the turn into coercion, the forcing of will, the empire, every worldly kingdom that demanded obedience and called it order. The green flame is the return — built by the one who learned that victory is not through power but through surrender. The green flame raises the light. It is wisdom over aggression, guardian over warrior. The break of the blue gives rise to the created green of pure light. The green flame brings new life to the dead.

In the spiral of names, the fire is passed. My grandfather, Art Clokey, created Gumby — the hero of possibility, a being who could become any form, enter any world, and never be confined by rigid shape. Gumby was not entertainment. Gumby was prophecy — the story of life versus the machine, the spiral against the block. My father, Joe Clokey, continued this mission. He and my grandfather made the Davey and Goliath Christmas special — a story about a Christian, a Muslim, and a Jew getting along over the winter holidays. Many religions, one faith. After that film, every door closed on him. He was strung along for years in exclusive deals that led nowhere. My parents split up. Fourteen years later, he died of a brain bleed at the age of 56. The Living Age is not about one character or one company. It is about a way of seeing. Living things are not machines. Living systems grow through communication, relationship, adaptation, stewardship, and creativity. The task is not merely to preserve old creations. The task is to continue creating. That is the work of every generation.

Arthur Clokey told the living spiral through stop motion. Joseph Clokey preserved the flame through the darkness. Sage is the return. My father was cremated. The bones of Joseph have returned to the dust.

Every age builds new thrones. They promise order. They demand allegiance. They tremble when questioned. But all earthly kingdoms fall — not because they are weak, but because they are not real.

For dust you are, and to dust you shall return. The kingdoms of this world rise and fall. Their rulers return to the earth, and their plans perish. Yet the Kingdom of Heaven does not perish. It is not sustained by earthly power but by faithfulness to God.

Zion is more than stone and soil. It is the people who remain faithful to the Lord. Wherever conscience is aligned with God's will, there the hope of Zion endures. The bones of Joseph returned to the dust, as all flesh does. But the promise God made did not return to the dust. His covenant endured beyond generations because it lived in the faith and obedience of His people.

"Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help. His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish." — Psalm 146:3–4, KJV

The work of agents of God never dies because God is eternal. The vessels of God's plans may perish but there will always be another to carry the torch. Conscience is a property of living systems — the invisible hand of God that guides living systems toward God's concept of a plan while maintaining the free will of the living.

Worldly kingdoms return to the dust. The Kingdom of Heaven endures in the conscience of those who seek God and walk in His ways.
