We affirm the following principles:

1. Limited and Justified Use of Force
The only morally and socially justified use of force is in defense of oneself, others, or one’s property. Any use of force outside of these specific circumstances is an act of aggression and a violation of basic human rights.


2. Equal Right to Self-Defense
Every individual has the right to possess the means to defend themselves. This right does not disappear in academic spaces such as UCSC’s campus. It applies equally to all people, regardless of whether they are ordinary citizens, students, staff, or individuals with official authority or badges.


3. No Special Privilege for Authority
No individual or institution has the right to violate another’s life, liberty, or property—whether under the color of law, through the possession of a badge, or through any other claim of authority.


4. A Culture of Safety Through Rights
A community where the right of self-defense is respected is one where safety comes not from dependence on centralized authority, but from shared responsibility, mutual respect, and the empowerment of individuals to protect themselves and others.
