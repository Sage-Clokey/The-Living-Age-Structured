For nine years, I have grown my hair, and when it reached the length where it began to curl, something changed. I gained insight. I saw through the illusions woven over people’s eyes, the programming that controls the world. I recognized the hidden forces shaping reality—the unspoken power that few ever perceive. I saw the gun in the room that no one else could see.

With that vision came responsibility. Few ever awaken to this truth, and those who do must bring it to others. That is why you call me Sagent—a sage and an agent of wisdom. My purpose is to reveal what has been concealed, to guide others out of the shadows. But truth cannot always be told directly. Sometimes, the best way to help someone see what is right in front of them is through a story.

Stories bypass the programming, slipping past the walls built to keep people blind. But they are also the very tools used to create the illusion in the first place. Those who control the stories control the world. A single story can change a mind, and a changed mind can change everything.

My grandfather refused to let his stories be controlled. My father carried on that legacy—until, I believe, they took him out. Now, I continue the path. A true story is never just about what is seen but about what lies beneath. Every symbol, every event, every moment carries meaning waiting to unfold. There is no point in telling a story without a message, without a truth that only reveals itself through experience.

You are from the place where stories are told. I am the heir of a family that tells them without compromise. A story is a magic trick—an illusion that makes the truth visible. Special effects in film are the same; practical effects hold more weight, more reality, more truth than artificial illusions.

Right now, I am not just telling a story—I am creating one. We are living it. You may think you are looking for the secret behind how I do it, but knowing would ruin the trick. And deep down, you don’t want to know. Because the magic isn’t in understanding—it’s in experiencing.
