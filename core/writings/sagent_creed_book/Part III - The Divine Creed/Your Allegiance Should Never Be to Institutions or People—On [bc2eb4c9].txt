Your Allegiance Should Never Be to Institutions or People—Only to Natural Principles

Laws change.
Governments fall.
Leaders rise and betray their followers.
Movements start with truth and end in corruption.

But natural principles never change.
And that is where true allegiance must lie.


---

1. The Trap of Loyalty to Institutions and People

People are flawed.
Institutions are corruptible.
Power shifts, and those who were once righteous become the very thing they swore to destroy.

The Jedi served the Republic until the Republic became the Empire.

Revolutions overthrow tyrants only to create new ones.

Political movements demand justice until they gain power—and then become the oppressors.


When you tie your identity to an institution, a leader, or a movement,
You risk falling when they fall, bending when they bend, becoming corrupt when they do.

The only way to stay true?
Anchor yourself to something that never changes.


---

2. Natural Principles – The Only Constant Truth

Natural principles are absolute.
They do not shift with politics, emotions, or popular opinion.

Truth remains truth, even when it is unpopular.

Justice is justice, even when the laws say otherwise.

Freedom is freedom, even when rulers call it rebellion.

Strength is strength, even when weakness is celebrated.


Governments will tell you right and wrong change with the times.
Society will say values must evolve.
Institutions will demand loyalty, even when they betray their own ideals.

But a Sagent does not serve governments, rulers, or organizations.
They serve truth.
They serve reality.
They serve the unbreakable laws of nature.


---

3. When the System Fails, Principles Remain

When the Republic fell,
The Jedi who remained loyal to the institution were destroyed.
But those who remained loyal to the Force survived.

When an empire collapses,
Those who serve the ruler are lost.
But those who serve justice and wisdom rebuild.

When the world crumbles, institutions will die.
But natural law cannot be erased.


---

4. The Path of the Sagent – Loyalty to Truth Alone

Do not pledge yourself to a flag that may one day stand for tyranny.

Do not worship a leader who may one day betray their ideals.

Do not follow a movement that can be twisted into the very thing it opposed.


Instead, stand firm in what is eternal.

If an institution no longer upholds justice, walk away.

If a leader demands loyalty over truth, they are not a leader.

If a movement sacrifices its principles for power, it was never real.


A Sagent does not swear loyalty to shifting sands.
They stand on solid ground—the unchanging laws of nature and truth.


---

Final Thought – The Only Allegiance That Matters

The world will always try to convince you that the rules have changed.
That morality is flexible.
That loyalty should be to a cause, even when the cause has lost its way.

But the Sagent does not follow the world.
The Sagent follows truth, discipline, and natural law.

And when institutions fall, when leaders fade, when movements crumble—
The Sagent remains standing.
