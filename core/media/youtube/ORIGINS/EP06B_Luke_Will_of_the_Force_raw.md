## Sage's Original Words (verbatim)

Luke Skywalker was given his father's lightsaber. The blue one representing a fighter. He joined because the rebellion needed a fighter to destroy the death star. But the force had other plans for Luke. He learned about the force from Yoda but he maintained his care for his friends and for that he lost his hand and the lightsaber he was given. So now he could do what the force wanted him to do. Be the one to unshaken the jar. The one to step away from the fight and see the humanity in the enemy. He would build his green lightsaber and then confront Vader but not in the way the rebels intended but in the way the force intended. In doing so he helped the force use anakin to bring balance. But he didn't fully understand the balance and created another Jedi order which was later destroyed by kylo ren and he went to the original Jedi temple to figure out what it meant to actually follow the will of the force. This is where he realized the force belongs to everyone not just the Jedi and it was time for the Jedi as an institution to end for the true light as balance to emerge. He make his final act to be to show the beginning of the true war the spiritual war and the rebirth of the rebellion. A rebellion not about fighting evil but bringing back living humanity. A Jedi uses the force for knowledge and defense never for attack. Lukes last action in the last jedi was about not fighting which fuels the dark some but showing the true spiritual power of the force.
