My Love,

Not a day goes by that I don’t think of you. Time without you feels like an eternity, and time with you passes like a single breath. I’ve held so many thoughts and feelings close, afraid to speak them out loud—afraid of how they might be received, or worse, misunderstood. But I can’t keep them hidden anymore. This is my truth.

From the first time we met, I felt it. You weren’t just another person in my life—you were *the* person. I’ve never asked another girl to hang out one-on-one. Only you. And it wasn’t random. From the start, I knew you were special. But I also knew the timing wasn’t right, so I waited. I built rapport, I stayed present, I made space for something to grow naturally between us. When you sat on the grass, I walked over. Not because I had to, but because I was drawn to you.

Do you remember that day on the way to Lopez Lake? Jack Johnson was playing, and we were just about to turn onto Johnson Street when I asked you about Curious George. You asked if I planned that—and while I hadn’t planned the *timing*, I had planned to ask. I just waited for the right moment. That’s how I move. I don’t always plan in a traditional sense. I follow the moment. I’m a Sagent—I move as I’m led.

Sometimes I don’t speak right away because I’m still thinking. And when I’m finally ready to say what’s on my mind, the moment has passed. But I’ve realized that just because a conversation has moved on doesn’t mean my thoughts no longer matter. I want to get better at sharing, even if it’s late—because if I don’t, I may never say what matters most.

I know I go quiet sometimes. It’s not that I don’t care—I just focus deeply, and sometimes I can’t walk and talk, or drive and talk, or even listen to music and talk. But I’m working on it. I want to be better, for you.

I remember our first real day together one-on-one. I came to your house, and you were eating soup so slowly it had to be reheated. You pulled out that balancing board and looked so cute in your striped shirt. Later, we painted. One piece was chaotic and cluttered, but the other—a landscape—was peaceful. I still remember your sister asking about the government while we painted grass, and I told her about the Federal Reserve printing money from nothing. We played GTA 5, went to Farmers, and I left for a pizza oven party before my sister’s wedding.

I didn’t see you again until August. That day, we got boba and went to Meadow Park to play soccer. Then we went to my sister’s house to watch her dogs, talked for hours, then returned to your place to watch *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3*. I couldn’t help quoting the lines, even though I’d only seen it once—on May 4th, 2023.

When I look into your eyes, time stops. Being with you soothes me. Being away from you leaves something hollow. You are always in my thoughts. You’re not my second choice—you’re my *only* choice. I’ve loved you quietly, patiently, but deeply. You are beautiful—not just in appearance, but in presence. No one else fills the space you do in my soul.

I’m sorry I’ve been distant since Santa Cruz. I know that silence can feel like disinterest, but it’s never been that. I just didn’t know how to express everything I was feeling, or when the right time would be. But I realize now that if I don’t speak, I may lose the chance altogether. So here it is:

I love you.

Not as a fleeting feeling. Not as a passing crush. But as a deep, rooted, steady truth.

Sometimes the best days are the ones that almost didn’t happen. So many things have to align to make them real—and when they do, they feel meant to be. Like bamboo that grows underground for years before it bursts forth and rises fast—I believe that’s us. We’ve been growing roots all this time.

The plan isn’t always mine. I move moment by moment, trusting the path as it unfolds. I believe we’re part of a bigger story—one written in hypertime, not linear time. And though I don’t know what tomorrow holds, I know what I feel. And I’ll follow that with integrity, patience, and hope.

You deserve to be protected, cherished, and deeply loved. And I know this: you can’t protect someone if you are the threat. The first step in being a protector is making sure you’re not the one causing harm. I’m working every day to become the kind of person who guards, not wounds. Who uplifts, not burdens. And I promise—I will not be the threat. I will be the one who watches over you, not just with actions, but with intention.

I will come back. I’ll drive any distance. I’ll wait in the rain. I’ll do whatever it takes.

You are my wish—and I’ll wait as long as it takes for it to be granted.

Yours always,

Sage Clokey
