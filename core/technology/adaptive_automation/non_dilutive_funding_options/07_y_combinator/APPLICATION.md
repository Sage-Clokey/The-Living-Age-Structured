LivingCAD translates plain English descriptions of desired biological function

living houses grown from engineered fungi.

### Why did you pick this idea to work on?
I'm a bioinformatics student who has spent years reading the code that makes
organisms what they are. Living organisms already produce materials stronger
than steel, harder than concrete, and self-repairing. The biology exists. The
design software — the tool that connects human intention to biological
possibility — doesn't. I'm building it because I'm the person standing at
the intersection of bioinformatics, language models, and materials science
who sees that it's possible now.

### What is your progress on the idea?
Working codebase — not a pitch deck:
- Genome retrieval: 220+ genomes from UCSC and NCBI
- Codon optimization: validated RSCU tables for 5 chassis organisms
  (Ganoderma lucidum, yeast, Arabidopsis, human, Komagataeibacter)
- Regulatory compatibility: 24 characterized parts, 5 organism classes
- Pathway conflict detection: 12 metabolic/signaling profiles
- Adaptive assembly engine
- Claude API integration for English → biological specification
- ESM-3 protein generation bridge: in progress (Phase 1 completion target)
