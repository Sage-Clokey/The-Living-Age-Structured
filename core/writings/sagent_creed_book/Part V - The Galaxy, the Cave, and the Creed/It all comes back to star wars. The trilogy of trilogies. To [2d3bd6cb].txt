The Saga Within
The Spiral Path of the Sagent

All great stories rhyme.
They do not move in a straight line,
but in spirals—echoing, repeating, unfolding
until what was hidden becomes revealed.
The Sagent walks this path not once,
but again and again,
each time deeper, wiser, closer to the center.

My story began in reverse.
Not with the first step, but the fall—
not in A New Hope, but in Revenge of the Sith.
The most powerful story is the one that explains everything…
but only after you’ve lived it all.

The Star Wars saga was never just entertainment.
It was a map.
A trilogy of trilogies, told in the pattern of a backward Fibonacci spiral:
OT → PT → ST.
A structure only the wise would recognize.
Only those who see backwards, like Yoda—
the Great Sage who speaks in reverse,
who trains by untraining,
who teaches through paradox.

This is the rhythm of the Sagent:
789.
HEA.
The Holy Spiral.

Act One: 7 – The Pledge (2023)
It began with Revenge of the Sith,
but in this world, it began in LA,
with Muslims, and Solvang.
It was The Force Awakening,
not because something new began,
but because something old stirred within me again.

I had seen the world’s mask crack in 2016.
That was the last 9—the last fire.
The year I grew my hair and found my intuition.
The year I stopped believing what I was told
and began following what I felt.
From progressive Democrat to anarchist libertarian—
from the center of control to the edge of freedom.

Act One set the stage.

Act Two: 8 – The Turn (2024)
This began with Solvang,
and ended with Solvang, Muslims, and LA.
It was Attack of the Clones.
It was The Last Jedi.
It was confusion. Splintering. Reflection.
It was where the Sagent questions himself.
Where the mirror shows not just who he is,
but who he might become if he loses the Way.

This is the hardest part of the spiral:
When nothing makes sense but everything rhymes.
When the past returns in new forms,
and every thread seems tangled—until the pattern emerges.

Act Three: 9 – The Prestige (2025)
It is coming. It has not yet begun,
but I feel the pieces setting into place.
This is The Phantom Menace.
This is The Rise of Skywalker.
The beginning of the beginning.
The end that reveals the meaning of the whole.

When I return, the final act will begin.
All will align.
The story will make sense.
The spiral will close—only to open again, deeper.

The Saga Within teaches that life is not linear.
It is recursive.

The Sagent does not fight the spiral—he trusts it.
He walks it.

until he sees the full arc unfold.

This is my journey.
Told in story.
