Brand Guidelines

  • Colors: Living green, gold, deep purple, midnight black, parchment cream
  • Typography: Georgia or similar serif. Never sans-serif for primary text. The voice is grounded, not corporate.
  • Symbols: Green flame, Fibonacci spiral, clay texture, DNA helix
  • Philosophy: Every product should be real, practical, and carry meaning. No empty logo merch. If someone picks it up, they should learn something or feel something.

Priority Tiers

  • Start here: Green Flame stickers, "Gate Has No Fence" tee, Field Notebook, Enamel Pin
  • Next wave: Living Spiral Hoodie, Declaration Print, Quote Poster Series, Coffee Mug
  • Premium / Limited: Declaration Broadsheet, Clay Gumby Kit, Seed Kit, Trilogy boxed set

Print-on-Demand Options

  • Apparel & mugs: Printful, Printify, or Gooten (integrates with Shopify)
  • Stickers: StickerMule, StickerGiant
  • Posters & prints: Printful or local print shop for premium runs
  • Notebooks & books: Lulu, BookBaby, or KDP for print-on-demand
