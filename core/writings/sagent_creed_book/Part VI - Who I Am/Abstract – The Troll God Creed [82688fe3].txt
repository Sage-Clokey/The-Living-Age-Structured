Abstract – The Troll God Creed

The Troll God Creed is a doctrine of subversive enlightenment—a sacred philosophy of disruption, irony, and perception warfare. Where the Sagent walks with clarity and reason, the Troll God dances in the blind spots of power, turning the system's own tools against it.

The Troll God is not a mere jester or provocateur. They are the embodiment of controlled chaos. A divine trickster who understands that the system thrives on seriousness, predictability, and belief—and so they wield absurdity, misdirection, and satire to expose the rot beneath the rituals.

The Creed holds that no empire falls from frontal assault, but from within. No tyrant can stand when their followers begin to laugh. The Troll God doesn't destroy by force. They destabilize by revealing contradictions, feeding the illusion until it collapses under its own weight.

To be a disciple of the Troll God is to weaponize the punchline, to see truth in paradox, and to embrace mischief as a form of moral clarity. Their actions are often misunderstood, dismissed, or feared—because they threaten not just power, but the narrative that gives power legitimacy.

Where the Sagent speaks, the Troll God winks.
Where others rage, the Troll God laughs.
And in that laughter, kingdoms tremble.

Because when the joke is on the system, the system begins to unravel.
