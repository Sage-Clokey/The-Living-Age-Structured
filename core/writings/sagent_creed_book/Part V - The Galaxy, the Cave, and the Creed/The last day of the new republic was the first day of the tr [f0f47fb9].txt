The last day of the new republic was the first day of the true Republic. The new republics strength wasnt at the core systems but is was the build of defense from each unit down to the individual towns. It wasnt built top down like the republic of old. It was decentralized and it's power came from the individuals and the core helped build the network of decentralized defense but the core was not central authority but a helper to build up the individual communities so that they could lead themselves instead of being at the mercy of the core worlds. So when the capital of the new republic was destroyed it actually make the true Republic stronget because it no longer had a point of weakness. It became the true Republic that served only the force.
