If you strike me down I shall become more powerful than you can possibly imagine. 

The greatest tool of the dark side of the force is the light side fighting it. Conflict fuels the darkside of the force and blinds the light. Compassion fuels the light side and brings light to the dark. No large scale battle can defeat the dark side. Once it gets to the big battle the dark side has already won no matter the outcome of the battle. The war against the dark side cannot be won with lightsabers and blasters it can only be won with The Force. When Luke tried to kill Palpatine he wanted him to. When you defeat the darkside by way of the sword you become the new dark lord. Later when Luke faced the first order without laying a blow and sacrificed himself the resistance didn't understand what he was doing. They believed that he was just stalling so they could live to fight another day. But that wasn't what he was doing. He went into hiding because he realized after Ben solo as kylo Ren destroyed the new Jedi order that the dark side has to be defeated with the light side not the dark. But this was blind sighted by Palpatines plan to intentionally create a large scale battle. He created such a large threat and game them a specific time horizon and a doable wild goose chase to find their location so that they would have an urgency and need to fight him. And he succeeded in this plan he got them to find him and fight, him and bring a lot of people to the fight and he also got his granddaughter to strike him down and kill him just like what he said she was going to do and that he would take control of her body and fuel the dark side in her.
