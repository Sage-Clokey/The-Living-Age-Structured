It is dangerous to see true reality when others are left in the dark. They will hate you for destroying their fantasies. This is why you must show them true reality in a fantasy that shows them the truth in the only thing they understand fantasy and lies. A lie can be comfortable until it is shown to be a lie. Once it is all foundations are gone and nothing seems stable because everything you knew was based on nothing. Sometimes when everything you see is a lie you must feel through fantasy of what you are shown and see the set pieces that put it together and break through the canvas that has been put before your eyes to blind you from the truth. We work in the dark to serve the light. We wander as Clokey through the dark to show the light in the one thing people understand performance.
