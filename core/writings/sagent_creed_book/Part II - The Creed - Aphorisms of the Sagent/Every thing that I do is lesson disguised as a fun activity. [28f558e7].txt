Every thing that I do is lesson disguised as a fun activity. There is a philosophy under every game. Karate isn't about fighting it is about peace and the need for strength to be peaceful. You must be capable of fighting but able to keep it sheethed unless absolutely necessary. Fighting comes out of weakness not strength. Anger comes out of weakness not strength. Strength is calm even in crisis. Weakness panics at crisis because it isnt strong enough to wait.
