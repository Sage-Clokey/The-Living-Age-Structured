### Trip 2: Approaching Humanity -> FEE -> YALCON (Chain Trip)

The smart move is to chain events 2-4 into one trip instead of flying back to SLO between each. This saves 4 flights and likely $400+.

| Leg | Route | Fly Date | Arrive For | Notes |
|-----|-------|----------|------------|-------|
| A | SBP -> PHX | Jul 17 (Thu) | Approaching Humanity starts Jul 17 at 7pm | Morning flight gets you there with time to spare |
| B | PHX -> BWI or DCA | Jul 19 (Sun) | FEE starts Jul 20 | Fly day after Approaching Humanity ends; BWI often cheaper, DCA closer to UMD |
| C | Explore DC | Jul 25-26 (Fri-Sat) | Free days after FEE | Monuments, museums, Capitol tours — UMD is right next to DC |
| D | DCA/BWI -> CVG | Jul 27 (Sun) | YALCON starts Jul 28 | Fly to Cincinnati the day before YALCON |
| E | CVG -> LAX -> SBP | Jul 30 (Thu) | Home | After YALCON ends |

### Recommended: Chain Route (no return to SLO)

```
SBP --> PHX --> BWI/DCA --> CVG --> SBP
Jul 17    Jul 19    Jul 25      Jul 30
```

Total flights: 4 legs (plus connections)
Away from home: Jul 17 - Jul 30 (~ 2 weeks)

### Alternative: Come Home for the Jul 25-28 Gap

```

Jul 17    Jul 19    Jul 24   Jul 27   Jul 30
```

Adds ~$150-250 for the extra round trip but gives you 2-3 days at home.

## Booking Notes

- **SBP is a small airport** — most routes connect through LAX, SFO, or PHX
- **Book as multi-city** (SBP -> PHX -> BWI -> CVG -> SBP) for the chain trip — often cheaper than 4 separate one-ways
- **BWI vs DCA for UMD**: DCA (Reagan) is closer (~15 min), BWI is ~30 min but usually cheaper. Either works.
- **CVG airport** is actually in northern Kentucky, about 15 min from Xavier University in Cincinnati
- **PHX**: Phoenix Sky Harbor, very well-connected hub — easy to get flights anywhere from there
