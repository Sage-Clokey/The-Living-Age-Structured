# Eukaryotic HGT — Papers to Read

## Downloaded

3. **Eyres et al. (2015)** — "Horizontal gene transfer in bdelloid rotifers is ancient, ongoing and more frequent in species from desiccating habitats." *BMC Biology*, 13, 90.

## Why These Matter

- Papers 1-3, 5: Cross-species and cross-kingdom HGT in eukaryotes — demolishes the claim that HGT is "just a prokaryote thing"
- Papers 4, 10: Bdelloid rotifers — 8-10% foreign DNA, used functionally against pathogens
- Papers 6-8: Intraspecific HGT — exosomes, nanotubes, mitochondrial transfer between cells of the same organism
- Paper 9: Microchimerism — whole-cell transfer between mother and fetus, persisting decades
- Together they prove the genome is an open ledger at every scale: between kingdoms, species, individuals, and cells
