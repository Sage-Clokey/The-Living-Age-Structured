The Forging of the True Republic

America was born in rebellion against kings and rulers.
Its founders cast off the crown, declaring that no man was born to rule another.
Thus was created the Democratic Republic.

But the seed was flawed.
For while it rejected monarchs, it embraced majority rule.
It replaced the tyranny of one with the tyranny of the many.
The crown was not destroyed—only multiplied.

True liberty was not fulfilled, only promised.
The parchment spoke of freedom, but the mob still chose the flavor for all.
The Republic became corrupted because it did not rise from individual choice,

Now the work remains.
We must take the idea and forge it to what it was meant to be.

The True Republic is not mob rule.
It is voluntary interaction between all people,
where no man is master, and no man is slave.
Where everyone is equal to all under God’s divine law,
the law written on every soul.

This is the Republic that cannot be corrupted—
because it is not the creation of parchment or power,
but the reflection of truth itself.
