Why do people play games that seem to have real life affect. What does it accomplish how is it advantageous? Every lesson must be in a story and the greatest story is the one where you a part of that story and that is what a game is. Learning to play a game teaches more than how to play that game it teaches you how to approach life from that games example. This is why my brother said that baseball practice was a replacement for church. It is a mechanism to show people how to live through metaphor. How you decide to play a game reflects how you play life. It trains you for when things are real with real consequences. Every pitch trains your mindset.
