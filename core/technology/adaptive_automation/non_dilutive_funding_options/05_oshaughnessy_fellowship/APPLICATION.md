## Application Narrative

### What are you building and why does it matter?

**DRAFT:**

I am building LivingCAD — the first organism-scale biodesign platform that
translates plain English into synthesizable DNA sequences for structural
organisms. The near-term product is design software for synthetic biologists.
The long-term product is living houses grown from engineered fungi.

Everything civilization builds is dead. Concrete, steel, glass — extracted,
processed, fixed, degrading from installation. The $10 trillion global
construction industry depends entirely on dead materials.

Living organisms already produce materials stronger than steel (spider silk),
harder than concrete (coral biomineralization), and more adaptive than any
engineered composite (fungal mycelium that grows around obstacles and repairs
damage). The biology exists. What doesn't exist is the design software.

LivingCAD connects human intention to biological possibility. You type:
"design a protein that cross-links fungal cell walls under mechanical stress."
The system interprets the biological intent (via Claude, Anthropic's LLM),
generates candidate protein sequences (via ESM-3, a protein language model),
optimizes the DNA for the target organism, validates against a compatibility
engine, and explains the result in plain English.

This is working code, not a pitch deck. The foundation includes a genome
retrieval system querying 220+ genomes, a codon optimization engine for
5 chassis organisms, regulatory element compatibility analysis, pathway
conflict detection, and an adaptive assembly engine.

The core infrastructure will be open source — the Red Hat model. Any iGEM team,
any small lab, any researcher in any country gets access to the same design
tools as a team at MIT. The most important designs won't come from corporate
labs. They'll come from independent researchers, small teams, and builders in
places where shipping concrete is impossible but growing mycelium is free.

This matters because it changes who gets to build. That's civilization-level.
