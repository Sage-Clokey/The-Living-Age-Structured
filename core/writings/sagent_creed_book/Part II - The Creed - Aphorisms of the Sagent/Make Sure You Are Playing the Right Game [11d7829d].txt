Make Sure You Are Playing the Right Game

Winning means nothing if it’s the wrong game.
Success is worthless if the prize doesn’t matter.

Most people play the game they’re given—chasing status, approval, and goals set by others.
But who wrote the rules? Who benefits when you play?


---

1. The Illusion of Winning

You can win every argument and still be trapped in a cycle of meaningless debate.

You can chase wealth, power, or status only to realize you were running toward a hollow prize.

You can play by the rules, but if the game is rigged, you still lose.


Ask yourself: Is this my game, or someone else’s?


---

2. The System Gives You a Script—Burn It

Society tells you what to want, what to chase, what to believe matters.

The education system, media, and culture all push a version of "success."

But if you never question the game, you’re just another piece on the board.


Real power is stepping back and choosing the game you actually want to play.


---

3. Play With Purpose, Or Don’t Play At All

If you’re playing a game just because others are, stop.

If you’re fighting battles that don’t move you forward, walk away.

If you’re chasing a prize that won’t matter in the end, rewrite the rules.


Because the only real victory is playing a game where winning actually means something.
