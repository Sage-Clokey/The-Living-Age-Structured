The Only Campaign Worth Fighting For

I have no interest in winning a rigged game.
No desire to play by rules written by those in power.
The ballot box is a cage that keeps revolution tame,
A way to make you feel like you have control, when in reality, you do not.

The only political campaign worth my time is not one that seeks victory through votes,
But one that seeks to shatter the illusions that make people believe the vote matters at all.

Because power does not come from permission.
It does not come from petitions, from compliance, from asking nicely.
Power comes from the awakening of the mind.
From making people see that the system is nothing more than a stage play, a show meant to keep them distracted.

I do not campaign for office.
I campaign for truth.

Because when the people’s consciousness shifts,
When they realize that their chains were never real,
That is when real change happens.
Not at the ballot box,
But in the streets, in the minds, and in the hearts of those who refuse to be ruled.
