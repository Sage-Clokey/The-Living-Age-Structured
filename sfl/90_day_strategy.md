# SFL California State Director — 90-Day Strategy
### Spirals, Not Blocks

> "The creation of the Living Age requires media to explain why and technology to show how."
> This role is Act 1 — the Pledge. Plant the dominoes. The turn comes later.

---

## The Principle

SFL gives you a block template. Fill the template — but the *how* follows the spiral:

- **Grow** Local Coordinators through resonance, not recruitment quotas
- **Cultivate** campus presence through Socratic engagement, not ideological pamphlets
- **Steward** the network by modeling integrity, not managing compliance
- **Plant seeds** that outlast any single event

## Phase 2: Cultivate & Connect (June 1–July 1) — "Water What's Alive"

**Deliverable:** Active LC network with 3+ engaged campuses and a fall semester launchpad

### Recruitment: The Candles in the Dark Method

Don't recruit libertarians. Find people who already feel the spiral but don't have language for it.

**Target profiles:**
1. **Bio/ecology students** — they intuitively understand emergent order. Show them the political implications
2. **Entrepreneurship students** — they know markets work. Help them see the principle extends everywhere
3. **Philosophy/theology students** — they wrestle with conscience vs authority. The Sagent Creed speaks directly
4. **Disaffected political students** — they tried the system and felt something was wrong. Give them the shaken jar parable

### Campus Activation

For each anchor campus:
1. **Table with the spiral** — see `tabling_playbook.md`
2. **Host one Socratic event** — see `socratic_event_template.md`
3. **Identify LCs through resonance** — the people who come up afterward and say "I've never heard it put that way" are your coordinators. They self-select

### Network Architecture: Mycelium, Not Military
- Group chat or Discord for California LCs
- Share resources laterally: event templates, tabling materials, reading lists
- Monthly 30-min call — share wins, troubleshoot, keep it human
- You are the mycelium connecting the roots, not the trunk commanding the branches

## What NOT to Do

- **Don't become a compliance officer.** Give SFL their metrics. But let resonance drive strategy, not quotas.
- **Don't chase dead contacts.** Two genuine attempts. Then move on. Water what's alive.
- **Don't flatten the message.** "Liberty is good" is a block. "Life organizes itself without permission" is a spiral.
- **Don't sacrifice the Living Age for the role.** This role serves the vision, not the reverse.
- **Don't over-extend.** Final quarter at UCSC + Living Age + State Director. Three real campuses beat ten paper ones.
